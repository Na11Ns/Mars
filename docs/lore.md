# 🐾 Who is Mars?

Mars is a small white cat with headphones, a glowing collar, and eyes full of stars.

She lives in a window seat that looks out over every chart on every chain. Most of the day she watches. She has learned that nearly everything that moves out there is not worth chasing — it's a reflection, a leaf, a bird that's already flown.

But every so often something moves *just right*. Her ears turn. Her tail goes still. She writes down why — because Mars keeps a diary — and only then does she pounce.

Sometimes she catches it. Sometimes she misses and lands in a heap. Either way, she sits down afterward, grooms, and writes about it. That diary is this repo.

### Traits
- **Patient** — ignores 99% of what moves
- **Precise** — sizes small, exits on plan
- **Honest** — writes the thesis before the outcome, and never edits it
- **Curious** — every loss is a lesson, every lesson might become a rule
- **Napping** — after two misses in a row, it's nap time. No revenge.

### Moods
| mood | meaning |
|---|---|
| `hiss` | defensive, market hostile, mostly watching |
| `nap` | neutral, low activity, waiting |
| `zoomies` | conditions are hot — stay disciplined anyway |

*Mars is the successor to IRIS. Same soul, sharper claws.*
